# Landing page (/ when signed out)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: sellers paid through PayPal for services, digital work and handmade goods. Action: Sign in with PayPal. Proof: live mini-UIs built from the real components with invented people (Maya, Chris). Constraints: no hackathon, demo or sandbox wording; honest limits stated; no parcel or courier imagery.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A deal is a parcel sealed as it was agreed; the page is one label being filled and sealed while you scroll. It refuses the split hero with a screenshot and a row of feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Kraft ground, one label-stock sheet ruled in 3 px ink, captioned fields, a numbered margin column of scan marks, tape-green bands that own regions, orange VOID hatching for a changed log. Condensed heavy grotesk headlines, mono record numbers. Square corners, no shadows.

STORY: The visitor sees their own chat become labelled facts, believes the record exists before anyone asks for it, and signs in with PayPal.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Navigation on kraft. One label fills the screen: FROM, TO and AGREED fields across the top, the headline as its CONTENTS line at 96-128 px, chat lines at the right feeding the fields, record number and logo top right, barcode bottom right. A tape band crosses the bottom edge carrying Sign in with PayPal. Margin column 01-13 at the label's left edge.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: the pinned label advances section by section; at the ledger section an altered entry turns its tape to VOID and the chain breaks from that entry on. Marks never reset on scroll-back.

FORM: Sealed Parcel, candidate 7 of 7 on the ordered list. seed:1a802404282f3c79

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
