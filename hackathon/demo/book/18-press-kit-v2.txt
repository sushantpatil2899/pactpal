Lanternlight press kit: about the game (version 2)
Pixel Harbor · written by Maya Lin Studio for Ethan Brooks

One line
A cozy adventure about keeping the lights on when the dark comes early.

About the game
Lanternlight follows Wren, the youngest lantern-keeper on the Hollow Coast. When the Long Dusk falls, she must keep forty hand-made lanterns burning until morning, guide lost travellers home, and find out what happened to the keeper who came before her. There is no combat and no timer, only a warm light, a long road and the people along it.

Key facts
Developer: Pixel Harbor, a two-person studio. Platform: PC (Steam). Length: about six hours. Languages at launch: English, with five more planned.

Quote
"We wanted a game that feels like walking home on a cold evening and seeing your window lit." (Ethan Brooks, Pixel Harbor)
