Harbour market: NPC dialogue (40 lines)
Lanternfall Games · written by Maya Lin Studio for Nora Quinn

FISHMONGER (BRAN)
1. "Fresh off the morning boat. Well, yesterday's morning."
2. "Mind the eels. They mind you."
3. "Lanterns out again on the north pier. Bad for trade, worse for sailors."
4. "You want the good herring, you come before the gulls do."
5. "My boy says the lighthouse keeper talks to herself. Says she answers too."

ROPE SELLER (ODA)
6. "Every knot tells you who tied it. Yours says hurry."
7. "Hemp for the boats, silk for the rich, and string for everyone else."
8. "Lost a whole crate to the tide. The sea takes its tax."
9. "If you're going past the cliffs, take more rope than you think."
10. "Come back when the wind turns. I'll have something special."

CHILD (PIP)
11. "Is it true the lanterns can see you?"
12. "I found a shell that sings. Want to hear? ...It's shy today."
13. "My gran says don't follow lights on the water."
14. "Race you to the fountain! I win. I always win."
15. "Can you make the dark go away? Just for a bit?"

GUARD (SERGEANT HOLM)
16. "Market closes at dusk. Which, lately, is always."
17. "Seen anyone carrying lamp oil in bulk? Asking for the harbourmaster."
18. "Move along, nothing to see. Except the fish. See the fish."
19. "Stay off the breakwater after dark. I don't fish people out twice."
20. "You've a keeper's look. Tired, but stubborn."

MERCHANT (VESNA)
21. "Spices from the south, cloth from the east, gossip from everywhere."
22. "Price is fair. Fair to me, at least."
23. "Buy two, I'll throw in a story."
24. "That map? Older than the harbour. Possibly wrong. Definitely pretty."
25. "Come closer. No, closer. There. Now you can see the real wares."

OLD SAILOR (GRIM)
26. "Forty years at sea and I never saw a fog like this one."
27. "The light on Hook Point went dark the night the Keeper vanished. Remember that."
28. "Don't trust a calm sea or a smiling captain."
29. "Buy me a drink and I'll tell you where the wreck lies."
30. "My knees know the weather before the gulls do."

BAKER (ILSE)
31. "Bread's warm, butter's cold, and the day's too short."
32. "Take a bun for the road. You look like you need it."
33. "Flour costs double since the boats stopped. Don't tell my customers."
34. "The keeper used to buy rye every Thursday. Haven't seen her in a month."
35. "Come back tomorrow, I'm trying a honey loaf."

AMBIENT BARKS
36. "Mind your step, the cobbles are slick!"
37. "Last call for the evening catch!"
38. "Did you hear? Another lantern went out."
39. "Two coppers, final offer."
40. "Light's fading. Pack up, pack up."
