Wren, the Lantern-Keeper: backstory and personality sheet
Lanternlight · written by Maya Lin Studio for Chris Park

Who she is
Wren Alder is sixteen and the youngest keeper of the Hollow Coast lanterns. Her grandmother kept the lanterns for forty years; when the old woman's eyes failed, Wren took the brass lantern and the long walk that came with it.

What she wants
To keep every lantern on the coast lit through the Long Dusk, so the fishing boats find their way home. Underneath: to prove the job is hers by choice, not by inheritance.

How she talks
Short sentences. Asks questions before she gives answers. Names every lantern ("Old Moss", "the Leaning One") and talks to them when she thinks nobody is listening.

Strengths
Patient. Notices small things: a cracked glass, a change in the wind, a lie. Never runs from the dark; she walks into it slowly, lantern first.

Flaws
Stubborn about doing things alone. Keeps promises she should not have made.

Three things she carries
The brass lantern (her grandmother's), a ring of keys to every lantern house, and a notebook of the names of everyone she has guided home.

Signature line
"Hold still. Let the light find you."
