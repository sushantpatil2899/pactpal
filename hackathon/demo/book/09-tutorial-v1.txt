Lanternlight: tutorial script and tooltips (version 1)
Written by Maya Lin Studio for Chris Park

Scene 1: The first lantern
WREN: "Old Moss again. Hold still. Let the light find you."
TOOLTIP: Hold [E] near a lantern to light it.

Scene 2: The wind
WREN: "Wind's turning. Shield the flame."
TOOLTIP: Stand between the wind and the lantern to keep it burning.

Scene 3: The traveller
TRAVELLER: "Which way to the village?"
WREN: "Follow the lights. I'll be right behind you."
TOOLTIP: Lit lanterns guide travellers. Light a path to the village.

Scene 4: The logbook
TOOLTIP: Press [L] to open the keeper's logbook. Every lantern you light is recorded here.
