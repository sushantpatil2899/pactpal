Lanternlight on Steam: localisation brief
Pixel Harbor · written by Maya Lin Studio for Ethan Brooks · draft for review

Languages (phase 1): French, German, Spanish (Latin America), Brazilian Portuguese, Japanese.

Tone
Warm, quiet and plain. Wren speaks in short sentences; keep them short. Avoid slang that dates quickly.

Names that stay in English
Wren, Hollow Coast, Lanternlight, Hook Point. Lantern nicknames ("Old Moss") are translated for meaning, with a note.

Text limits
Store short description: 300 characters in every language. Item names: 24 characters. Tooltips: 80 characters.

Deliverables from the translators
One spreadsheet per language, with a column for the translator's notes and a column for questions.

Questions for Ethan before sending to vendors
1. Should the goat have a name in every language?
2. Can Japanese use vertical text in the logbook screens?
