Short description (300 characters)
Walk the Hollow Coast as Wren, the youngest lantern-keeper, and keep the lights burning through the Long Dusk. A cozy adventure about small kindnesses, old secrets and finding your way home.

About this game
The Long Dusk has come to the Hollow Coast, and every lantern must stay lit until morning. As Wren, you will:
- Relight forty hand-made lanterns, each with a story of its own.
- Guide lost travellers, fishing boats and one very stubborn goat back to safety.
- Trade, mend and befriend your way through six seaside villages.
- Uncover what really happened to the keeper who came before you.

No combat, no timers: just a warm light, a long road and the people you meet along it.

Key features
- A hand-painted coast that changes as the dusk deepens.
- Gentle puzzles built around light, shadow and wind.
- A soundtrack of folk instruments and sea sounds.
- About six hours to finish, with secrets for completionists.
