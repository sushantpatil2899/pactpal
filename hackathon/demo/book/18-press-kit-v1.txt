Lanternlight press kit: about the game (version 1)
Pixel Harbor · written by Maya Lin Studio for Ethan Brooks

Lanternlight is a cozy adventure about Wren, the youngest lantern-keeper on the Hollow Coast. When the Long Dusk falls, she must keep forty lanterns burning until morning.

Developer: Pixel Harbor, a two-person studio.
Platforms: PC (Steam).
Price: to be announced.
