# Delivering work

## Send through a tracked link

Upload your files on the delivery step and send them. Your buyer gets an email with a link to the delivery. Each file is fingerprinted, and every time your buyer opens the delivery or downloads a file, the time, internet address and browser are recorded in the [evidence log](evidence-log.md).

Before you send, a pre-delivery check can compare the files with the agreement's checkable lines (format, size, what the picture shows). It is advice for you only.

## Approvals and revisions

Your buyer approves the delivery or asks for changes. Changes count against the revision rounds you agreed; the deal shows how many are used. An approval completes the step and is recorded with the rest.

## Physical goods

For small physical goods, paste your product's listing link on the agreement ("What's being sold"): PactPal reads the listing's details and photos, and your buyer signs them with the rest of the agreement.
