# Getting paid through PayPal

PactPal does not hold money. Your buyer pays you through PayPal, straight to your own PayPal account, one invoice for each agreed stage.

## Invoices for each stage

The signed agreement sets the stages (for example: a deposit, a payment when the sketch is approved, a final payment). When a payment step comes up on the timeline, choose **Send invoice**. The amount and due date come from the signed agreement, and the agreement's fingerprint is in the invoice's note.

Invoices go through your own PayPal app, which you link in **Settings** ([getting started](getting-started.md)).

## When your buyer pays

PayPal tells PactPal when an invoice is paid. The payment step is marked done, the payment is recorded in the [evidence log](evidence-log.md) and the next step can start. If a notification is slow, **Check payment status** on the deal asks PayPal directly.

A late payment shows on the timeline and holds up the steps after it. PactPal reminds your buyer before it is due and when it is overdue.

> PactPal currently runs in test mode. No real money moves.
