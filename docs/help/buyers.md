# For your buyer

Your buyer does not need a PactPal account.

## One link by email

When you send the agreement for review, your buyer gets an email with a link to their deal. From it they can:

- **Read every term on one page**: the work, what is not included, revisions, stages, payments and due dates.
- **Ask questions**. Answers come only from the agreement, citing the line; anything the agreement does not answer goes to you.
- **Sign** by typing their name and choosing **I agree and sign**, or **ask for changes** in their own words.
- **Chat** with you in the deal's chat.
- **Pay** each stage's PayPal invoice.
- **Open deliveries**, then approve them or ask for changes.

Your buyer can also sign in with PayPal from their link to keep access to their deals.

## What signing means

The page your buyer signs says what signing records: the time, their internet address and their browser, with a copy of the exact page. Both of you get a receipt by email. A typed name is not proof of legal identity.

## Disputes

A buyer who disagrees opens a dispute in PayPal, as they always could. PactPal never files anything for a buyer and never acts for one.
