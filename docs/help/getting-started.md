# Getting started

## 1. Sign in with PayPal

Choose **Sign in with PayPal** on the home page. PayPal shares your name, your email address and your PayPal account's id with PactPal; your password stays with PayPal. Signing in shows that you control a PayPal account. It is not proof of legal identity.

## 2. Link your PayPal app

Invoices and disputes go through your own PayPal app. Open **Settings → Your PayPal app** and add your app's client id and secret. PactPal checks them with PayPal, keeps the secret encrypted and never shows it again.

## 3. Make a deal

Choose **New deal** and start from wherever you agreed the work:

- **Paste** a chat or an email thread.
- **Upload** a saved chat or a file.
- **Forward** an email to your private PactPal address.
- **Send it from another tool** with a private key, or ask your [AI agent](ai-agents.md) to create it.
- **Chat in PactPal**: add your buyer and agree the work in the deal's own chat.

Pick what you are selling (services, digital work or small physical goods) and the currency.

## 4. Let the AI draft the agreement

Ask PactPal to draft the agreement. Five AI models each draft it from the chat, check each other and pick one whole draft ([how it works](ai-council.md)). Every term points to the chat line it came from, or stays blank. You read it, change what you like and confirm it.

## 5. Send it to your buyer

Add your buyer's email and send the agreement for review. Your buyer gets a link, reads every term on one page, can ask questions, and signs by typing their name, or asks for changes ([what your buyer sees](buyers.md)).

## 6. Work through the timeline

Once signed, the deal follows its [timeline](agreements.md): what the buyer provides, reviews, PayPal invoices for each stage and deliveries. PactPal reminds whoever a step is waiting for, and records each step in the [evidence log](evidence-log.md).

## Your pages

| Page          | What it shows                                                                   |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Dashboard** | What is waiting for you, open disputes, what is waiting for others, "Needs you" |
| **Deals**     | Every deal in a table you can filter, group and export                          |
| **Messages**  | Every deal's chat, grouped by the person you deal with                          |
| **Needs you** | Reminders and news that need an answer                                          |
| **Disputes**  | Open PayPal disputes and their deadlines                                        |
| **Deadlines** | Due steps and dispute deadlines across deals, on a calendar                     |
| **Insights**  | Charts of your deals, money and records, which you can rearrange                |
| **Settings**  | Your PayPal app and [your AI agent](ai-agents.md)                               |

Press **⌘K** (Ctrl+K) anywhere to jump to a deal or a page, and **⌘\\** (Ctrl+\\) to fold the sidebar away.
