# The problem, in sellers' own words

Evidence for the README's problem section and the demo video's opening. Every figure has a link. Every screenshot is of a public page, captured on 11 Nov 2026.

## The short version

PayPal decides a dispute on the evidence it gets. For digital goods and services, PayPal's own rules ask for "a system of record". Most small sellers have no such record. Their proof lives in chats, emails and screenshots, and they lose. Buyers say the same thing from the other side: PayPal took the seller's word, because nothing showed what both sides had agreed. The pattern shows up in posts from 2012 to 2026.

## How the images were made

- Each image was captured from the live page in a headless browser.
- Before each capture, names, avatars, profile links, @mentions and PayPal case numbers were blurred on the page. No unblurred copy was saved.
- The grey line at the top of each image (site, kind of post, date) was added to say where the post came from. The post text is unchanged.
- Long posts are cut down to the paragraphs that matter. Each image links back to the full post above.
- Sites that blocked automated reading (Reddit, PayPal's Community forum) were skipped. No log-ins were used and no site checks were bypassed.
