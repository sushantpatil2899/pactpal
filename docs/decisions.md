# Decisions

A running log of design decisions and the reasons behind them. Newest decisions are added at the bottom of each section.

## Product

| Decision                                                                                       | Reason                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Name: **PactPal**                                                                              | Follows PayPal's two-part pattern (what you do + why you trust it). No conflicting product or USPTO mark found; `pactpal.com` was available at the time of checking.                                                 |
| Primary user is the **seller** (freelancer, creator, small business paid via PayPal)           | Buyers open disputes; sellers are the ones who need a defense. Buyer features are advisory only.                                                                                                                     |
| Core idea: create evidence **at the time of the deal**, not after a dispute opens              | Existing dispute tools rebuild evidence after the fact. Records made with the buyer's explicit agreement are stronger and cheaper.                                                                                   |
| No escrow, no holding funds                                                                    | PayPal's user agreement states PayPal is not an escrow agent, and holding funds raises money-transmission questions. Milestone invoices give the same structure.                                                     |
| No buyer-side claim filing                                                                     | Buyers must file claims themselves in PayPal's Resolution Center.                                                                                                                                                    |
| Never promise outcomes                                                                         | The product reports "evidence completeness", never "you will win" or that Seller Protection applies.                                                                                                                 |
| Sellers can use PactPal for **services and digital work** (focus) and **small physical goods** | These are where PayPal disputes reach sellers directly and proof of scope, delivery and approval is hardest. Physical goods with tracking already have stronger protection, so they are supported but not the focus. |
| **Deposit bookings, retainers and regulated services are blocked** ("not supported yet")       | More dispute-prone and rule-heavy (cancellation terms, professional regulation). On the roadmap.                                                                                                                     |
| PayPal-prohibited categories and high-risk extras are blocked                                  | Gift cards and stored value, crypto, NFTs and investments, vehicles and real estate, plus everything in PayPal's Acceptable Use Policy. See [scope.md](scope.md).                                                    |

## AI

| Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Reason                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| AI drafts, humans decide                                                                                                                                                                                        | AI never creates evidence, timestamps or approvals. Every send to a buyer or to PayPal needs explicit confirmation.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Citations or blank                                                                                                                                                                                              | Every AI-drafted field or sentence must cite a source line or ledger event id; otherwise it stays blank. Code checks this.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| AI across the whole deal                                                                                                                                                                                        | Scope drafting from chat, pre-delivery scope check, message watcher for scope creep, buyer Q&A answered only from the agreement, claim-vs-record breakdown when a dispute opens, appeal check.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **LLM council** for high-stakes tasks (scope extraction, claim-vs-record, appeal statement)                                                                                                                     | Independent models cross-check each other. Answers failing code validation are dropped, the rest are anonymized and ranked by every member, a Borda count picks one whole answer, and low agreement flags the result for human review. Prior work: Karpathy's LLM Council and Mixture-of-Agents research.                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Single fast model for frequent tasks (message watcher, buyer Q&A)                                                                                                                                               | Latency and cost.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Mixed council** of different model families, called through hosted APIs only (Gemini, Qwen and gpt-oss on Groq, Ministral on Mistral, Nemotron on OpenRouter)                                                 | Diversity across model families cross-checks better than several copies of one. Gemini is closed and is paid from the developer's credits; the others are open-weight models run by hosting companies, and some have free tiers. The registry accepts any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, so models can be swapped by a config edit. Nothing runs on our own server (docs/scope.md). Replaces the earlier "open-model council" plan.                                                                                                 |
| Measure, don't claim                                                                                                                                                                                            | Evals compare single-model and council results on a fixed test set before any claim is made in the submission.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **AI category check**                                                                                                                                                                                           | While drafting the scope, the AI also classifies what is actually being sold from the conversation. A mismatch with the seller's chosen category is flagged; a blocked category stops the deal. The notice is logged in the ledger.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Model list as of sub-phase 3a: `gemini` (gemini-3.6-flash), `qwen-groq` (qwen/qwen3.8-27b), `gpt-oss-groq` (openai/gpt-oss-120b), `mistral` (ministral-14b-latest), `nemotron-openrouter` (a free NVIDIA model) | Checked against each host's live model list and with `pnpm ai:check` on 31 Oct 2026. Groq no longer lists any Llama chat model and OpenRouter has no free Qwen, so Llama is not in the registry. Gemini 2.5 Flash is closed to new users, and Gemini 3.8 Flash did not answer within 40 s (3.6 answers in about 2 s). Mistral's free key allows 0 requests a minute for mistral-small and medium, but 30 for ministral-14b. A model takes part only when its key is set; the council size (3 to 5) is chosen by the evals in 3g. |

## PayPal integration

| Decision                                                                                                                                                                                  | Reason                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **MCP first, REST fallback**                                                                                                                                                              | Each operation tries a PayPal MCP tool first (Agent Toolkit or the hosted sandbox MCP) and falls back to REST if the tool is missing, errors or times out. Operations without an MCP tool (evidence, offers, appeals, messages, webhook verification) use REST directly. The transport used is recorded and shown.                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Sellers connect their own PayPal account                                                                                                                                                  | Main path: Partner Referrals ("Connect with PayPal") with invoicing and dispute permissions, calling on the seller's behalf with `PayPal-Auth-Assertion` (payer id, not email). Fallback: the seller connects their own sandbox REST app.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Webhooks as the source of truth, polling as backup                                                                                                                                        | Dispute webhooks may not reach a platform for every seller; the dispute list is polled per seller as a backup.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Read allowed actions from PayPal every time                                                                                                                                               | Allowed actions and offer types change with dispute state; never assume them. Dispute writes are sent one at a time.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| US sandbox accounts                                                                                                                                                                       | Sandbox capabilities vary by country; US accounts support the full flow.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Test disputes via a second business account                                                                                                                                               | A sandbox business account acting as the buyer can create disputes through its own REST app. Packaged as a sandbox seed script.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Access tokens cached in memory per app, renewed 5 minutes before expiry; concurrent callers share one login; a rejected token is renewed once                                             | Tokens last about nine hours. Renewing early avoids a token expiring mid-request, and sharing one login avoids a burst of logins when many requests start together.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Every PayPal call: 15-second time limit, up to 3 tries with exponential backoff and jitter, retrying only network errors, 429 and 5xx                                                     | Transient sandbox failures (for example intermittent 500s on refunds) are common. Client errors (4xx) are never retried because repeating them cannot succeed.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Writes must carry a stable `PayPal-Request-Id`, enforced by the types; retries reuse it                                                                                                   | PayPal treats repeats with the same id as one request, so a retry after a lost answer can never create a second invoice, refund or evidence upload.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| An app's permissions are read from its login answer (`pnpm sandbox:check`)                                                                                                                | The token response lists every granted permission, so feature checks need no extra calls and change nothing in the sandbox.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| PayPal MCP is reached over SSE (`https://mcp.sandbox.paypal.com/sse`) with the official MCP SDK                                                                                           | The documented streamable HTTP address returned 404 while `/sse` connected. The SDK marks SSE as deprecated, so the address is one setting to change when PayPal's HTTP endpoint works.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| MCP attempts have a 10-second limit; after 3 MCP failures in a row MCP is skipped for 10 minutes                                                                                          | While MCP is broken, every call would otherwise wait on it first. Each result records `mcp` or `rest` and the reason for falling back.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| A write falls back to REST only when MCP certainly did not do it (no connection, or PayPal answered with an error); after a timeout or an unreadable answer the write fails instead       | PayPal's MCP tools accept no request id, so REST could not recognise a repeat and might create a second invoice or upload. Reads always fall back.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Invoice amounts are passed as whole minor units with a currency code and converted to PayPal's decimal strings only at the PayPal boundary (no decimals for JPY, HUF, TWD)                | Matches the money rule in the engineering standards and avoids floating-point errors.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| One invoice per milestone is created and then sent as two writes with fixed request ids (`<id>` and `<id>-send`); a failed send reports the created invoice id                            | Both steps can be retried safely, and a created-but-unsent invoice is never lost or duplicated.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| The scope reference is written into the invoice note as "Agreed scope: …"                                                                                                                 | The buyer sees what the payment is for, and the invoice links back to the signed scope in disputes.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Dispute listing follows PayPal's next links up to 10 pages of 50, and says when it stopped early                                                                                          | Nothing is silently missed, and a broken pagination answer cannot loop forever.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Dispute reasons, statuses, stages and outcomes are kept as PayPal's own text, not a fixed list                                                                                            | PayPal adds values over time; an unknown value is shown rather than rejected.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| What the seller may do on a dispute is read from its action links each time (all but `self`), and offer types from `allowed_response_options.make_offer`                                  | Allowed actions change with the dispute's state and must never be assumed.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Every dispute answer (evidence, offer, accepting the claim, appeal) re-reads the dispute first and is refused unless PayPal allows that action, and for offers that offer type, right now | A refused answer costs nothing; a wrong one sent to PayPal is hard to undo.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Answers to the same dispute are queued and sent one at a time; a refused or failed answer does not block the next                                                                         | PayPal briefly locks a dispute after each seller write and refuses a second one.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Evidence and appeal files are checked by their first bytes (PDF, PNG, JPEG, GIF), must have a plain name, and must be 1 byte to 10 MB                                                     | A renamed file cannot slip through, and bad files are reported before any upload.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Accepting a claim tries PayPal's MCP tool only for a full refund with a note of letters, digits, `_` or `-`; other answers use REST directly                                              | The MCP tool takes no refund amount and only that note format; evidence, offers and appeals have no MCP tool.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| `pnpm sandbox:respond` is a dry run unless `--send` is given                                                                                                                              | Nothing goes to PayPal without a person explicitly choosing to send it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| The server is hosted on Render, described in `render.yaml` (a Blueprint), deploying the `dev` branch on each commit                                                                       | Render is a hackathon sponsor with its own track, PayPal needs a public address for notifications, and a Blueprint keeps hosting versioned and repeatable.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Starter instance (always on, 0.5 CPU, 512 MB), paid from the hackathon's Render credit                                                                                                    | The free instance sleeps after 15 minutes and takes about a minute to wake, delaying PayPal notifications and demos.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Render region: Virginia (US East)                                                                                                                                                         | Nearest to the developer (US) and to PayPal's US systems; a Render service's region cannot be changed after it is created.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| The test buyer pays sandbox invoices by hand in a browser; `pnpm seed:sandbox` waits up to 15 minutes and can be resumed with `--invoice`                                                 | PayPal has no API for a payer to pay an invoice; browser automation would need the buyer's password stored as a secret and may be stopped by PayPal's login checks.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| The seed command finds the buyer's transaction by transaction search, with `--buyer-transaction` to give it by hand                                                                       | Search needs no extra secrets, but it lags by hours, so the by-hand path is the reliable one for now.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Opening a test dispute uses a request id fixed per invoice (`seed-dispute-<invoice id>`)                                                                                                  | Re-running the command can never open a second dispute for the same payment.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| PayPal error messages include PayPal's field and issue codes (never their descriptions or values)                                                                                         | The cause of a rejected request (for example `MISSING_ISSUE_TYPE`) is visible at once without leaking submitted data.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Webhook verification sends PayPal the received body byte for byte inside the verify request                                                                                               | Re-serialising the event can change its bytes and make genuine notifications fail verification.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Webhook answers: 200 handled or repeat, 400 not genuine or malformed, 500 PayPal could not be asked (so PayPal retries), 503 not configured                                               | PayPal retries non-2xx answers; only temporary problems should be retried.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Every genuine PayPal notification is saved in full in `webhook_events`, by source and event id, before the server answers 200; if saving fails the server answers 500 so PayPal retries   | An event we have not saved is never acknowledged, repeats are recognised after restarts and deploys, and two copies arriving at once cannot both count as new (the database decides in one step; tested on real Postgres). The whole body is kept so later handlers can run, and be re-run, without asking PayPal again; it holds buyer details, so the retention rule (Phase 8) applies.                                                                                                                                                        |
| The server bundle includes the npm libraries of workspace packages; only the server's own dependencies stay external, and the build fails if any external import cannot be resolved       | pnpm keeps each package's dependencies private, so the built server could not find the MCP library; this was caught before the first Render deploy.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Phase 1 decision gate: dispute verdicts and appeals are shown **for real** in the sandbox, not as labelled replays                                                                        | Done end to end on 29–30 October 2026: escalate, evidence, the sandbox-only verdict (`adjudicate`) and an appeal all worked on real sandbox disputes (`PP-R-SEI-10195178` won by the seller, `PP-R-EMM-10195224` won by the buyer and appealed). The verdict step only appears 12–25 minutes after evidence, so demo data must be prepared ahead of time. A won appeal stays open for the buyer's ~27-hour response window, so demo data with a finished appeal must be prepared at least a day ahead (or that final step is a labelled replay). |
| Disputes are found by polling the seller's dispute list as well as by webhooks                                                                                                            | No `CUSTOMER.DISPUTE.*` notification arrived for either test dispute, at any stage, while invoice notifications did.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Evidence uploads use an evidence type the dispute requests from the seller                                                                                                                | PayPal refuses other types with `EVIDENCE_TYPE_IS_NOT_ALLOWED`; the requested types are listed on the dispute.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Sandbox-only dispute steps (`require-evidence`, `adjudicate`) live in a separate `PayPalSandboxDisputes` class used only by the test commands                                             | They stand in for PayPal's staff and must never be called by the product; they share the same allowed-action check and one-at-a-time sending as real answers.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |

## Engineering

| Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Reason                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| TypeScript end to end (React + Vite, Node + Fastify, Postgres)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | One language; first-class support in the partner tools and the PayPal Agent Toolkit.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Hash-chained, append-only ledger                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Each entry includes the previous entry's hash, so edits, backdating and deletions are detectable. Corrections are new events.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| MIT license                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Simple and widely understood.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Written engineering standards with an automated gate (`pnpm verify`)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Production-grade quality must not depend on memory. Formatting, types, strict lint, coverage, duplicate code, architecture boundaries and dependency vulnerabilities are checked by tools; see [engineering-standards.md](engineering-standards.md).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| CI runs the same gate on every push and pull request                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | The standard holds regardless of who or what writes the code.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Settings are checked once at startup; wrong values stop the server, missing values only mark the features that need them as not ready                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | A bad value fails loudly at the start instead of deep inside a PayPal call, while the server can still run before every key is added. `pnpm env:check` shows the status without printing values.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Secret settings are wrapped so printing, logging or serializing them shows `[redacted]`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Secrets must never reach logs, even by accident.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| The PayPal API address is fixed to the sandbox, not a setting                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Live money is out of scope ([scope.md](scope.md)); a wrong setting must never reach real PayPal.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| The server is bundled with esbuild, and the build is part of `pnpm verify`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Packages stay TypeScript source with no build of their own; bundling compiles them into the server's runnable output, while npm dependencies stay external so the lockfile and audit still cover them. Building in verify catches a broken build before it reaches hosting.                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Postgres, run as Render's managed database, is the database                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | The data is linked records (sellers, deals, milestones, files) that must be saved together with their ledger entry, so it needs real transactions, constraints (one ledger entry per deal and position, no edits or deletes) and exact money types. Render manages only Postgres and Key Value (a cache); MongoDB or MySQL would have to be self-run on a disk, which limits the service to one copy and blocks deploys without downtime. SQLite has the same one-disk limit.                        |
| Uploaded files are kept in Postgres, behind a small interface, until they outgrow it                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Ledger and files then share one backup and recovery point, and every Render service can reach them (a Render disk belongs to one service only). Each file's SHA-256 is written to the tamper-evident ledger, so integrity never depends on where the bytes live. Limits: 10 MB per file and 50 MB per deal, to protect the 1 GB database. Move to object storage (S3-style) when stored files near the database's storage allowance or files larger than 10 MB are needed; only the adapter changes. |
| The disputes and packets tables are created in Phase 5, not Phase 2                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Their shape depends on Phase 5's design; Phase 2 creates only tables it uses and tests.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Database changes are numbered migrations in code, with a checksum, run under a database lock by a command Render runs before each deploy                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | An edited migration is refused, two runners cannot collide, and a failed migration stops the deploy while the running version stays up. Forward-only, as the engineering standards require.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| `/health` only says the server is running; `/ready` also checks the database and migrations                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Render restarts a server whose health check fails, which would not help when only the database is down. `/health` lists only the settings the running server needs.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| The Render database is the smallest paid plan (0.1 CPU, 256 MB, 1 GB storage), in Virginia, Postgres 16, reachable only on Render's private network                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Enough for the hackathon and cheap; the plan can be upgraded and storage can grow, but region and version cannot change after creation. Version 16 matches the Postgres used in CI.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Tests use an in-process Postgres (PGlite); CI also runs one integration test against a real Postgres 16                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | `pnpm verify` needs no database installed, while the real server's behaviour (connections, locks, the `pg` driver) is still checked on every push.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Everyone signs in with PayPal ("Log in with PayPal"); a buyer may instead just use an emailed link, with no account                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Sellers are paid through PayPal anyway, no email service or password is needed, and the PayPal payer id is needed in Phase 6. Buyers keep a no-friction path but can also sign in, so one person can be a buyer in one deal and a seller in another. To be confirmed with a live sandbox check when it is built.                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| One `users` table for everyone; seller and buyer are roles on a deal                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | A buyer can later sell, and sign in with PayPal if they choose. A person invited by email is a user without PayPal ids and is linked later only when the email is proven both ways. See [data-model.md](data-model.md).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| A user has one free-text `display_name`, not first and last name                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Names do not split cleanly worldwide; the legally relevant name is what the buyer types when agreeing (kept in the ledger), and PayPal handles invoice names. Collects less personal data.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| A deal has a `kind` (`service`, `digital`, `physical`), a `category`, one `currency`, and a `status` that is only a convenience copy                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | `kind` decides how delivery is proven and maps to PayPal's SERVICE or PRODUCT dispute type; the category list will change so it is checked in code; one currency keeps all milestone invoices consistent; the ledger stays the truth.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Ledger entries name the acting user, covered by the entry hash; the store refuses a `seller` or `buyer` entry from anyone who is not that deal's seller or buyer                                                                                                                                                                                          | "The seller did this" must mean a specific, checkable person. Entries without an acting user hash exactly as before.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| A new `DEAL_CREATED` event; creating a deal and its first entry are one transaction                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | A deal never exists without its first ledger entry.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Ledger IP addresses are stored as text, not Postgres's `inet` type                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | `inet` rewrites addresses, which would change the text that was hashed.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| The ledger is protected by the database (no update, delete or truncate; no deleting a deal or user that has entries) and by verification                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | The database rules stop mistakes and application bugs; the hash chain detects changes by anyone with full access. Writers to one deal take turns through a row lock, with the (deal, position) key as backstop; tested against a real Postgres.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Each file's bytes are stored once, by SHA-256 (`file_contents`); `artifacts` holds the per-deal record; both are append-only                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | The same file added twice is stored once, and the fingerprint is the address, so reading can prove the bytes are the recorded file. Corrections are new artifacts, never edits. Deleting files for privacy requests is a Phase 8 retention question.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Adding a file saves the bytes first, then writes the ledger entry and the record in one transaction; early checks run before anything is stored, and the deal's size total is re-checked under the deal lock                                                                                                                                              | A record never points at missing bytes, a refused upload stores nothing, and two uploads at once cannot exceed the limit (tested on real Postgres). A failure can only leave an unused stored copy. This is also the order an object-storage adapter needs. `artifacts` has no database link to `file_contents`, so the store can be swapped.                                                                                                                                                        |
| A file's type comes from its first bytes (PDF, PNG, JPEG, GIF), else `text/plain` if it is plain UTF-8 text, else `application/octet-stream`; never from its name                                                                                                                                                                                         | A renamed file cannot pass as something else. The same byte checks and name rule now serve PayPal evidence uploads and deal files (one copy, in `packages/ledger`). The download route (Phase 4) must force a download and never show a delivered file inline.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Webhooks require the database; the in-memory store was deleted                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | One path to maintain. Without `DATABASE_URL` the route answers 503 and PayPal retries.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| `webhook_events` has no append-only guard                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Later phases will mark events processed, and a retention purge may need to delete; the ledger, not this table, is the evidence record.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| The server serves the web pages from its own address (`pactpal-server.onrender.com`); the browser never holds PayPal tokens                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | The current guidance for browser apps that sign in through a provider ("backend for frontend"): the server talks to PayPal and the browser keeps only a sign-in cookie. Separate `*.onrender.com` addresses count as different sites, so the cookie could not be shared and a token would have to live in page code. A custom domain later keeps the same design.                                                                                                                                    |
| Sign-in uses PayPal's code flow with a one-time `state` value in a 10-minute cookie; the code is swapped once (never retried) at Log in with PayPal's token service, `POST /v1/identity/openidconnect/tokenservice`, and the person is read from `GET /v1/identity/oauth2/userinfo?schema=paypalv1.1`; PayPal's key for the person is then dropped        | `state` stops other sites from completing a sign-in in someone's name; a code works only once; PactPal needs the identity, not access to the person's account. This is the only combination PayPal accepted in live tests: the general `/v1/oauth2/token` gives an app-style key that the identity addresses decline, and the other userinfo addresses declined every key. See [sandbox-notes.md](sandbox-notes.md).                                                                                 |
| The person's details are read from either shape PayPal documents (`sub`/`email`/`email_verified`, or `user_id`/`emails`)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | PayPal's documentation shows both and does not say which the sandbox returns; to be confirmed by the live check.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| The session cookie is `__Host-pactpal_session`: HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Lax, Path=/, 7 days; a fresh random token every sign-in; only its SHA-256 is stored                                                                                                                                                                                            | Page scripts cannot read it, it travels only over HTTPS and only with our own site's requests (and the return from PayPal), and a leaked sessions table cannot be used to sign in.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| The seller app (`PAYPAL_SELLER_*`) is used for "Log in with PayPal" until Phase 6                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | It stands in for PactPal's own PayPal app until the platform app takes over. Its Return URL is `https://pactpal-server.onrender.com/auth/paypal/callback`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
